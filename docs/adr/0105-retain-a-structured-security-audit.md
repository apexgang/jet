# Retain a structured security audit

`jetd` maintains an owner-only structured Security audit for authentication, Pairing and authentication failures, approval and policy decisions, Path grants, Craft and Harness-extension changes, recovery, exports, and destructive operations. Each record contains time, Plane, Actor, target, decision, risk, and outcome but no credential, prompt, terminal output, or file content. Records are retained for one year by default with a configurable minimum of ninety days. Conversation deletion retains only this content-free metadata until audit expiry, replaces its target with an opaque deleted-target identifier, and discloses that fact in the deletion preview; Authority fences and the Deletion ledger retain their independent safety lifetimes. Canonical records form an integrity chain whose durable head remains outside rollback-capable SQLite state. Validation failure enters Security-degraded mode: reads, export, and existing Runs continue, but new Pairing, policy and Craft changes, and destructive operations are blocked until the interactive owner exports the evidence and explicitly begins a new audit epoch containing a recorded gap. Jet does not claim resistance to malicious code already executing as the same operating-system user, and remote log forwarding is outside v1. This applies ASVS 16.1.1, 16.2.1 through 16.2.5, 16.3.1 through 16.3.4, 16.4.1, and 16.4.2; Jet deliberately does not claim ASVS 16.4.3 in v1.
