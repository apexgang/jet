# Isolate Crafts and Runs in separate processes

`jetd` runs one out-of-process instance per active Jet Craft artifact digest to multiplex Runs pinned to that exact Adapter, while one generic `jetfueld` owns each individual Harness process, its I/O, and its replay spool. Updating a Craft stages a new default digest for new Runs without replacing the Adapter underneath active work. Normal disable or removal blocks new Runs and waits for pinned Runs to finish. A user-only Force disable terminates the Craft, leaves its Harness under `jetfueld`, and marks affected Runs as needing attention. A digest revoked through signed Jet release metadata is force-disabled automatically and cannot restart; v1 maintains no centralized third-party denylist. Otherwise a crashed Craft restarts at the same digest with bounded backoff and resumes from helper source offsets, and Jet never silently upgrades an active Run. Bundled and third-party Crafts use the same protocol, and `jetfueld` keeps Runs alive while either their Craft or `jetd` reconnects.
