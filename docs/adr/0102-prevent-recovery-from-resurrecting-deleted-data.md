# Prevent Recovery from resurrecting deleted data

Permanent deletion removes live Jet state and eligible Artifacts but may leave content in bounded Recovery snapshots until their disclosed expiry. Before acknowledging the deletion, `jetd` synchronizes a content-free, append-only Deletion ledger under owner-only `~/.jet/recovery`, outside SQLite and rollback-capable snapshots. Its monotonic records contain only deleted identities and times, integrity-chain data, and an atomically advanced durable head; they remain until every snapshot that could contain the deleted identity has expired. Restoring a local database snapshot always reapplies the current ledger rather than rolling it back. If the ledger or its head is missing or corrupt and the candidate snapshot may predate a deletion, Jet refuses writable restoration and remains in Recovery mode. A separate interactive Recovery purge creates and verifies a post-deletion snapshot and then removes every older affected snapshot; Harnesses, Crafts, schedules, Utility models, and Autodelete rules cannot invoke it. This makes retention explicit and enforces ASVS 14.2.4 and 14.2.7 without silently sacrificing all recovery history on routine cleanup.
